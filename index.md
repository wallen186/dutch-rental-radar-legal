# Dutch Rental Radar

### Legal Information & Privacy

Dutch Rental Radar is a private, invite-only application designed to help a household discover, evaluate, and compare long-term rental properties in the Netherlands.

The application helps its users organize rental listings, compare housing costs, evaluate commuting options, and manage their apartment search.

Dutch Rental Radar is operated for personal, non-commercial use and hosted on the Base44 platform.

## Privacy & Access

The main application is private and available only to authorized users. Rental listings, household information, notes, and preferences are not published on this website.
